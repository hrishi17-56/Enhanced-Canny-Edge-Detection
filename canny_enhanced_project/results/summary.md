## Metric notes

Precision is the share of detected edge pixels near a true edge. Recall is the share of true edge pixels that were found. F1 combines them. All three use a 2 px tolerance and no one-to-one matching, and they are only available for the drawn scenes. Drawn scenes are cleaner and simpler than photographs, so scores above do not predict photo accuracy. Run time of this script: 40 s.
